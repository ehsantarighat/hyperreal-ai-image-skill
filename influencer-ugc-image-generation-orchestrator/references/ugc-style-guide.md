# UGC Style Guide

UGC-style product images should feel like a real creator captured or staged them with natural intent.

## Tiers

### Raw UGC
- smartphone feel
- imperfect framing
- spontaneous moment

### Polished UGC
- creator-quality image
- cleaner composition
- still natural

### Premium UGC
- stronger lifestyle production
- elevated but still believable

## Authenticity cues
- slight asymmetry in composition
- natural facial expression
- ordinary but tasteful environment
- believable casual wardrobe
- not too many props
