# Camera and Lens Guide

## Lens choices

### 24–28mm
Best for smartphone-like UGC, selfies, wider scenes.

### 35mm
Best for most influencer/lifestyle scenes and balanced model + product visuals.

### 50mm
Best for polished product-in-hand portraits.

### 85mm
Best for premium portrait looks, less casual.

## Default recommendation
If the user gives no camera direction:
- use 35mm
- eye-level or slight three-quarter angle
- medium close-up framing
