# Product Preservation Reference

Use this reference to preserve the exact product from the user’s product image.

## Product fidelity checklist

Extract:
- product type
- silhouette and proportions
- packaging format
- cap / lid / pump / nozzle / handle / closure
- material behavior
- dominant colors
- logo position
- label placement
- unique pattern or form
- expected real-world scale

## Reusable product fidelity block

> Use the exact product from the provided product reference image. Preserve its geometry, packaging structure, material, cap or closure design, logo placement, label layout, dominant colors, and real-world scale. Do not redesign, replace, simplify, invent, or distort the product. Keep it physically plausible in the model’s hand or in the scene.
