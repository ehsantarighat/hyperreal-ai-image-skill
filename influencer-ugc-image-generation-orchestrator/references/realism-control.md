# Realism Control Reference

Use this file whenever the image must not look AI-generated.

## Core realism dimensions

### Skin realism
Require:
- visible natural skin texture
- subtle tonal variation
- natural lip texture
- no excessive smoothing

Avoid:
- plastic skin
- over-retouched beauty filter
- waxy cheeks
- poreless complexion

### Hand realism
Require:
- realistic finger count
- believable joints
- natural grip
- correct overlap with product
- plausible wrist angle

Avoid:
- fused fingers
- extra fingers
- melted knuckles
- floating grip

### Lighting realism
Require:
- motivated light source
- coherent shadow direction
- believable reflections
- realistic highlight strength

Avoid:
- impossible light directions
- over-cinematic glow
- glossy studio look when UGC is desired

## Realism language block

> Hyperrealistic real-world photography. Natural skin texture with subtle imperfections, realistic hair strands, physically plausible hand anatomy and product contact, consistent shadows, believable surface reflections, natural fabric folds, realistic background depth, and no synthetic CGI polish.
