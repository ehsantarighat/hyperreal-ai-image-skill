# Prompt Modules Reference

## Identity Module Template
[Name], a [age appearance]-year-old [gender presentation] with [face shape], [skin tone], [eye shape] [eye color] eyes, [eyebrow description], [nose description], [lip description], [hair length] [hair color] [hair texture] hair, [body type], [makeup style], [typical expression], [style personality]

## Realism Enhancers
- realistic skin texture
- visible pores
- subtle imperfections
- soft under-eye texture
- natural facial asymmetry
- realistic hands
- natural body posture
- soft flyaway hair
- detailed fabric texture
- authentic candid moment
- natural color grading

## Fake-looking Avoidance
- avoid plastic skin
- avoid waxy face
- avoid perfect symmetry
- avoid distorted hands
- avoid extra fingers
- avoid fake smile
- avoid broken anatomy
- avoid inconsistent shadows
- avoid warped background
- avoid text artifacts

## Lens Guide
- 35mm: street, lifestyle, full-body, environmental, candid
- 50mm: natural, balanced, human perspective, lifestyle portrait
- 85mm: close-up, portrait, beauty, shallow depth of field
- smartphone: UGC, selfie, casual, imperfect, handheld

## Lighting Guide
- Window light: soft, believable, indoor lifestyle
- Golden hour: warm, emotional, outdoor lifestyle
- Overcast: realistic, muted, street fashion
- Direct flash: raw, nightlife, UGC, candid
- Studio softbox: polished, beauty, editorial
- Ambient restaurant: intimate, evening lifestyle
